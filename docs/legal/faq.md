## What does supporting get me?

Encrypted sync across your devices, encrypted backup and restore, and a small supporter heart. It's $6, $12 or $24 a year, or $30 once, tax included, and every amount gets the same features. Everything else stays free for everyone, with no account.

## What if I lose my sync code?

If sync is still on for one of your devices, open sync settings there and choose **Show my sync code**. If you've lost every device and the 24 words, your synced menus can't be recovered, by you or by us. To stop a yearly charge in that case, cancel at [link.com](https://link.com) or ask [Link support](https://support.link.com/topics/sold-through-link).

## Can you read my menus?

Not the synced ones: they're encrypted on your device, and we never get the key. Menu **links** are different. A link contains the menu, so anyone who has it can read it, and our web host receives it when the link is opened.

## How do I cancel or change my amount?

Choose **Manage subscription** in the app, or go to [link.com](https://link.com). Cancelling keeps sync until the paid year ends. A higher amount applies now (charged pro rata), and a lower one applies at renewal. Refunds: `[FILL IN: one-line summary of your refund policy]`.

## How do I delete everything?

Choose **Delete my synced data**. It cancels your subscription and deletes your synced menus and account at once. It also ends lifetime status, so be sure. Menus on your devices stay until you delete them or clear the site's data. Stripe and Link keep payment records; you can [ask them](https://support.link.com/topics/sold-through-link) to delete your information.

## Why is the seller "Link"?

We use Stripe Managed Payments, where Stripe's Link service is the merchant of record. It handles checkout, tax and receipts, which lets a one-person project sell in many countries. So your receipt says "Sold through Link", and your card statement shows `LINK.COM* [FILL IN: statement descriptor]`.

## Does it work on my phone?

Yes, it runs in your phone's browser, and there's no app store app. Sync needs Safari 17, Chrome 137 or Firefox 129, or newer. On iPhone, Safari can delete a site's saved data if you don't visit for a while, so consider **Add to Home Screen**. The Home Screen version keeps its own separate storage, so menus saved in Safari don't appear there by themselves.

## What happens if I stop supporting?

Menus on your devices stay and everything free keeps working. Your synced copies become read-only: you can still download them, but new changes aren't uploaded. Twelve months after lapsing they're deleted. The app warns you 30 days before, but only inside the app, because we don't have your email.

## Is this therapy?

No. It's a tool for conversations, not therapy or medical advice. In the US, call or text **988** for crisis support, or call the National Domestic Violence Hotline at **1-800-799-7233** (text **START** to **88788**). In an emergency, call your local emergency number.
